Privacy and cookies

What data are processed, for what purpose and what your rights are. This site uses no cookies.

This English version is provided for information; the Spanish version prevails.

Summary

  • This site uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts: your browser connects to Google servers, which receive your IP address. Google Fonts sets no cookies.
  • Personal data are processed only when you send them by email or through a form.

Data controller

Euskal Herriko Unibertsitatea / University of the Basque Country (EHU), through the Hologenomiks research group. Barrio Sarriena s/n, 48940 Leioa (Bizkaia), Spain. Group contact: adrian.odriozola@ehu.eus. EHU Data Protection Officer: dpd@ehu.eus.

What data and why

When you request information, pre-registration, an analysis or a quote, we process the data you provide (name, email and, where applicable, phone, organisation and request details) in order to reply and, if you so request, prepare the service.

  • Legal basis: your consent and pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).
  • Retention: as long as needed to handle the request. If no service is contracted, data are deleted within one year of the last communication.
  • Recipients: data are not disclosed to third parties unless required by law.

Analysis data

If you order a genetic or microbiome analysis, sample and analysis data are processed under a specific informed consent provided separately, in accordance with the GDPR, Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 and Law 14/2007 on biomedical research. Genetic data are identified by a sample code and stored separately from personal data.

Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw consent at any time, by writing to adrian.odriozola@ehu.eus or to the EHU Data Protection Officer (dpd@ehu.eus), or through the forms at ehu.eus/babestu. You may also lodge a complaint with the Basque Data Protection Authority.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage in your browser; therefore it shows no cookie banner.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web server, technically logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
