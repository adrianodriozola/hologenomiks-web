EHU research group

Human genomics and microbiome applied to precision health

We study the human holobiont, the host genome and its microbiome as a single system, and how it responds to diet, physical activity and circadian rhythms.

We translate that evidence into practice through university training, interpreted genetic and microbiome analyses, and R&D projects with institutions and companies.

How it is applied

Genomic and microbiome data become useful when they are integrated with each person's or project's history, habits and goals.

Nutrition and health

Dietary guidance informed by the individual profile

The microbiome profile and relevant genetic variants help professionals prioritise foods, fibres and probiotics, and measure the response to the intervention.

Physical activity and sport

Training, recovery and gut health

Genetic markers of training response and microbiome dynamics during competition provide context to plan load, nutrition and rest.

Research and industry

Evidence for products and interventions

Studies with long-read sequencing and bioinformatic analysis to evaluate foods, probiotics and intervention strategies with methodological rigour.

A university research group

Hologenomiks belongs to the Department of Genetics, Physical Anthropology and Animal Physiology of the Faculty of Science and Technology of the EHU, on the Leioa campus.

Training is delivered as official university microcredentials, and services are contracted through the university's Technology Transfer Office (OTRI).

Group leader

Prof. Dr. Adrian Odriozola Martínez

Associate Professor of Genetics at the EHU. He studies the interaction between host genetics and the microbiome and its application in nutrition, physical activity and prevention. Author of 75 scientific articles, inventor of 8 patents and editor of two volumes of Advances in Genetics (Elsevier).
