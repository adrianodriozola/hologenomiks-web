Who we are

The group

An EHU research group that studies the human genome and its microbiome, trains professionals and transfers its results to industry and society through services, collaborative R&D projects and competitive funding calls.

Director and principal investigator

Prof. Dr. Adrian Odriozola Martínez

Associate Professor of Genetics at the Faculty of Science and Technology of the EHU, accredited as Full Professor (Unibasq, 2025).

PhD in Biochemistry from the EHU with an Extraordinary Doctorate Award. He began his career in forensic and population genetics, where he developed genetic identification systems, and for more than a decade has studied the interaction between host genetics and the gut microbiome and its application in nutrition, physical activity, circadian rhythms and colorectal cancer prevention.

He leads the Hologenomiks group and the university microcredential in genomic and microbiome analysis, and is the contact person for the group's training, analyses and collaborations.

A working network

The group combines basic and applied research with the training of new researchers. PhD theses, bachelor's and master's projects and internships are carried out in the genetics of performance and sports injuries, the microbiome and physical performance, precision nutrition and the microbiota associated with colorectal cancer.

We work in a network with universities and research groups, technology centres, hospitals and biobanks, and with companies in the biohealth sector (biotechnology, biomedicine and digital health), the agri-food and nutrition sector, and physical activity and sports performance.

This network makes it possible to run complete projects, from sample collection and custody to sequencing, bioinformatic analysis, validation and transfer of results, and to build multidisciplinary consortia for regional, national and European R&D&I calls.
