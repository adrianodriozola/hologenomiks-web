Who we are

The group

An EHU research group that studies the human genome and its microbiome, trains professionals and transfers its results to industry and society through services, collaborative R&D projects and competitive funding calls.

Director and principal investigator

Prof. Dr. Adrian Odriozola Martínez

Associate Professor of Genetics at the Faculty of Science and Technology of the EHU, accredited as Full Professor (Unibasq, 2025). Director of the Hologenomiks group at the Department of Genetics, Physical Anthropology and Animal Physiology.

PhD in Biochemistry from the EHU with an Extraordinary Doctorate Award. He holds two recognised research periods (sexenios) and is certified as a Health Biologist by the Spanish General Council of Official Associations of Biologists.

He began his career in forensic and population genetics, where he developed genetic identification systems. For more than a decade he has studied the interaction between host genetics and the gut microbiome. He applies it to nutrition, physical activity, circadian rhythms, healthy ageing and colorectal cancer prevention.

He has been principal investigator of R&D projects with companies and public administrations. He is an inventor on Spanish, European and international patents in human genetics and precision nutrition. He received the Basque Award for Technological Innovation (Toribio Echevarria Award, 2017).

Since 2013 he has gained entrepreneurial experience in biotechnology applied to health and sport, and in knowledge and patent management in the biosciences. He directs the EKIN48 entrepreneurship programme of the Faculty of Science and Technology, with ZITEK: 16 editions and more than 310 projects mentored.

He directs the university microcredential in genomic and microbiome analysis. He co-directed the EHU postgraduate programme in Chemistry and Genetics in Health, Nutrition and Sport (2015-2021). He is a visiting lecturer in genetics applied to nutrition and sport at universities in Spain and Latin America.

He is the contact person for the group's training, analyses and collaborations.

A working network

The group combines basic and applied research with the training of new researchers, and works in a network with institutions and companies. This structure makes it possible to run complete projects, from sample collection to validation and transfer of results, and to build consortia for regional, national and European R&D&I calls.
