Services and developments

Microbiome analyses and genetic analyses with reports interpreted according to the available evidence, and technical and scientific support for research projects and companies. All services are provided and invoiced through the EHU.

Analyses for individuals and professionals

Designed so that a health, nutrition or sport professional, or the user themselves, has individual biological information with which to guide and measure an intervention.

Microbiome

Gut microbiome analysis

€220VAT included

We characterise the gut microbiome from a stool sample by full-length 16S rRNA sequencing, which resolves many taxa to species level.

How it works

  1. Kit sent to the user's homeTube with preservative, instructions and informed consent.
  2. Sampling and shippingThe sample is stable at room temperature for two months and is sent by post.
  3. Sequencing and bioinformaticsDNA extraction, full-length 16S, taxonomic classification and comparison with reference values for each phenotype. Application of bioinformatic systems developed by the group itself.
  4. ReportsUsually 10 to 15 working days from sample arrival (maximum 20).

What it is for

To assess the state of the gut ecosystem at a given time (diversity, functional groups, potential to produce metabolites such as short-chain fatty acids, presence of probiotics), guide diet and supplementation changes with a professional, and compare progress after an intervention.

What you receive

  • Results report by category: gut health, general health, metabolism and energy, diet comparison, physical activity, probiotic foods, probiotics and postbiotics (short-chain fatty acids, vitamins and neurotransmitters).
  • Recommendation report with a personalised ranking of foods, prebiotics and probiotics.
  • Optional: sports performance report · €250 (VAT included).

Host genetics

Genetic analysis from raw data

€150VAT included

If you already have a consumer genetic test (23andMe, MyHeritage or similar), we interpret your raw data file without a new sample: more than 180 traits related to nutrition, metabolism, body composition, exercise response and recovery.

How it works

  1. RequestForm and specific informed consent.
  2. Secure file uploadThe raw data are identified by a sample code; personal data are stored separately.
  3. Quality control and interpretationEach variant is assessed against the scientific literature and reference databases.
  4. ReportResults by category, with the tendency of each trait and its level of evidence.

What it is for

To identify predispositions with practical use (nutrient tolerance, metabolism of compounds such as caffeine, response to each type of training) and, with a professional, prioritise decisions on diet, supplementation and training.

What you receive

  • Report with results grouped into categories: health, diet, nutrients, supplementation, blood tests, body composition, physical activity, endurance, power, and fatigue and recovery.
  • For each trait: genotype, tendency relative to the general population, explanation and level of evidence.
  • Technical sheet of the traits analysed.

The reports are not diagnostic tests and do not replace assessment by a health professional. They provide individual biological information to guide, together with a health, nutrition or sport professional, decisions on diet, supplementation, training and rest, and to measure the response to those decisions over time.

For research groups and companies

Custom sequencing and bioinformatic analysis

We work with research groups, healthcare centres and food, probiotic, sport and health companies on genomics and microbiome projects, from study design to analysis and publication.

  1. DesignQuestion, sample size and analysis plan.
  2. Samples and laboratoryCollection and stabilisation of stool or swabs, DNA extraction and quality control.
  3. SequencingLong reads with Oxford Nanopore (MinION): full-length 16S rRNA; other targets and metagenomics on request.
  4. Analysis and resultsTaxonomy, diversity, differential abundance, integration with clinical or physiological data, report and publication support.

Formats

Technical service with a fixed quote, or collaborative R&D project under a contract with the EHU managed by the OTRI. Data and results of each project are confidential.

Technology-based companies and centres

We work with technology-based companies, technology centres and healthcare organisations to develop and validate products, services and algorithms based on genomic and microbiome data. Transfer is formalised through contracts with the EHU, licences of our patented technology or joint projects.

R&D&I funding calls

We take part as scientific partner in consortia for competitive regional, national and European calls: experimental design, sequencing, bioinformatic analysis and dissemination. If you are preparing a proposal, please contact us well before the call deadline.

Developments

Our services incorporate tools developed from the group's research, mainly Phymofood, the patented method (EP 4 224 486 B1) that ranks foods by their potential to favour the low-abundance taxa of each person's microbiome.

The group leader is the inventor of 7 patents in applied genetics, genetic identification and precision nutrition.
